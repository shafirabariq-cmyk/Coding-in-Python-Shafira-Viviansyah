Design Rationale

I separated the app into layers (cli.py for prompts, validation.py for rules, services.py for calculations, storage.py for files) so that the business logic never calls input(), print() or touches a file. This lets me test rules such as "amount must be greater than 0" or "balances are never mixed across currencies" directly with pytest, without typing into a menu. All allowed values (kinds, categories, currencies) and error messages live in one module, validation.py, and both entry points (the CLI and the CSV import) use the same functions, so the rules cannot drift apart. One business decision: reports keep a separate balance per currency instead of adding EUR, USD and IDR together, because a single total across currencies would be a meaningless number without exchange rates. Undo removes the last transaction and immediately rewrites the file, so the file on disk always matches the true state, even after a crash and reload.
